Experience

I’m Anthony Bellomo, based in Milan. I work across visual design and digital content, with experience in high-volume asset production, quality checks and coordination between teams.

During my Strategic Account internship at Just Eat Takeaway, I produced and adapted over 100 digital assets a day for web and app use. The work combined retouching and composition with Cloudinary asset management, brand consistency checks and Jira coordination with production, account managers and marketing.

Approach

How I work

I helped standardise production through templates, Photoshop actions and naming conventions, and supported two new colleagues with internal processes. I also developed a bulk image-processing web app with AI coding tools and presented it to Operations for testing.

Alongside this experience, my personal and commissioned graphic work explores identity, typography, editorial layouts and social formats. I’m interested in digital content, content operations and web workflow roles that bring visual judgement and practical organisation together.
