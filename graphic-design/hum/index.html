The brief

An editorial voice for independent music.

HUM is a fictional independent music magazine. The brief was to create a complete visual identity and a consistent editorial presence across Instagram.

The system combines Roc Grotesk and Neue Haas Grotesk with black, white and acid yellow. Modular layouts carry the identity across five formats: brand tile, artist spotlight, cover reveal, feature preview and listening guide.
